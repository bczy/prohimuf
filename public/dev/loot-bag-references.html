🎨 UNIVERSE DIRECTIONS: Visual Moods

Different color/texture universes the 3D model could inhabit. Pick ONE direction per asset type.

A. MONOCHROME STREET: Black & Worn

Faded black nylon, scuffed leather, dirty canvas. The aesthetic of objects that have been through a hundred drops. Matte surfaces, visible wear marks, frayed edges. Rendered grayscale with neon rim applied by the shader.

worn textures matte black photocopied fanzine no logos

B. 90s SPORTSWEAR: Brand-adjacent

Generic 90s athletic aesthetic: navy blue, forest green, burgundy. Nylon with white or silver accents. Think bootleg Adidas, market-stall Nike, the bags everyone had. Clean but not new, lived-in.

nylon sheen generic stripes 90s palette no real brands

C. MILITARY SURPLUS: Olive & Khaki

Army surplus shop vibes: olive drab canvas, khaki webbing, brass buckles. The gear that ends up in squats and free parties. Rugged, utilitarian, slightly anachronistic (Cold War surplus in 1998).

heavy canvas metal hardware surplus store rugged

D. DISPOSABLE URBAN: Plastic & Paper

Cheap, throwaway containers: grocery bags, cardboard, black trash bags. The aesthetic of hiding value in plain sight. Crinkled plastic, bent corners, anonymous.

plastic sheen cardboard texture anonymous disposable

E. BUSINESS CLANDESTINE: Leather & Chrome

The upscale drug money aesthetic: black leather briefcases, chrome latches, serious business. Not flashy luxury: understated, functional elegance. The stuff that goes through airports.

matte leather chrome accents serious business 90s executive
