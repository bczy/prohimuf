# Story — Timer-duel télégraphié + grammaire de feedback d'époque (`S0.1`)

**Epic:** `socle-fidélité (veille 2026-07-18)` · **Sequence:** S0.1 · **Type:** core-loop fidelity refinement, cross-boundary (game + bridge + render).

## Why

Today an enemy fires the **same tick** it enters `SHOOTING` — there is no discrete "about to fire" beat. The green→red neon rim (`neonHeatColor.ts`, ADR-0025) is an *exposure* clock, not a *fire-imminence* cue. That makes `Éviter` a memorisation puzzle, not a reflex duel: the player cannot react to an incoming shot, only to elapsed time. Prohibition's descendants (Zapper, Virtua Cop — veille `docs/game-design/veille-concurrentielle-shooters.md` §2.2/§2.3) all telegraph the shot before it fires, so the player can win the exchange by reacting. We also have zero screenshake/hitstop anywhere in gameplay, a 200ms sustained white "flash" that reads as a tint rather than a flash, and no visible death-break on kill — the feedback grammar is not yet legible enough to sell the duel or make an innocent-hit mistake feel categorically bad. This story closes both gaps: it inserts the missing telegraph beat and sharpens the impact/death/error feedback around it, without adding any new player verb or widening the core loop.

## Cahier des charges check

> "Did Prohibition Atari ST have a telegraphed timer-duel with impact flash and clean death-break?"

**[FIDÈLE]** for the telegraph + white-flash-impact + clean death-break lineage — Prohibition/Zapper/Virtua Cop already play this exact duel (veille §2.2/§2.3); part of it is already in this codebase (impact flash, exposure rim, innocent penalty) and this story completes and corrects the missing beat rather than inventing one.

**[EXTENSION]** (conscious, justified) for **multi-magnitude screenshake + 60-80ms hitstop**. "Screenshake" as a formal discipline is a 2010s idiom (Vlambeer, 2012) — Prohibition never had it. It is kept because:
- `Éviter` — the telegraph is what makes avoidance fair at the mouse; shake/hitstop are pure legibility amplifiers of that same duel, they add zero new verb.
- `Récupérer` / `Livrer` — untouched; the loop is not widened, only its existing beats (hit / kill / error) are made more readable.
- Anti-anachronism guardrail respected: shake hits the **scene only**, never the crosshair/HUD, and the story explicitly bans FPS-genre tells not in period — no floating hit-marker cross, no kill-cam, no gradient health bar, no chromatic aberration (`docs/art-direction.md` §8.1 OUT).

## Scope (V1)

Covers, per veille §2.2/§2.3:
- A **crisis-flash telegraph** before every enemy shot (a new `AIMING` state between `VISIBLE` and `SHOOTING`) — the riposte window the player can win.
- A **per-target riposte window**: killing the enemy during `VISIBLE`/`AIMING` cancels its shot outright (it never reaches `SHOOTING`).
- **Impact = white flash on the struck silhouette, ≤2 frames**, then reverts to pose/death — not a sustained tint.
- **Death = a clean, visible silhouette break** (topple/drop/fade) before the enemy clears — never an instant blink-out.
- **Multi-magnitude screenshake + 60-80ms hitstop** that shakes the *scene*, never the crosshair/HUD.
- **Ultra-legible failure feedback** on an innocent hit — categorically distinct (largest tier + dedicated cue) from a plain miss.
- Bans (art-direction §8.1 OUT, veille anachronism guardrail): no floating hit-marker cross, no kill-cam, no gradient health bar, no chromatic aberration.
- A `reducedMotion` accessibility toggle so shake/hitstop/flash can be tempered without weakening the telegraph itself (the telegraph is the fairness contract, not a motion effect).

**Explicitly out of scope this story** (see "Out of scope" below): per-archetype telegraph durations, driver-kill/car mechanics, any change to `enemySpeedMultiplier`/difficulty ramp beyond re-measuring after the telegraph lands, trauma² shake decay curves, and fixing the pre-existing CRT toggle's `aria-pressed`/44px gaps (flagged, not silently inherited).

## Mechanic spec

**Telegraphed riposte (the duel core).** Insert a new enemy state `AIMING` between `VISIBLE` and `SHOOTING`. Cycle for a shooter becomes `HIDDEN → APPEARING → VISIBLE → AIMING(telegraphMs) → SHOOTING → HIDDEN`. The enemy bullet still spawns only on the edge into `SHOOTING` exactly as today (no wiring change) — the telegraph now always precedes it. The **riposte window** is the `AIMING` duration, **minus the final tick** (see AC2's boundary-tick clarification — the pre-hit snapshot invariant is preserved unchanged, so a kill landing on the exact tick `AIMING` elapses does not retroactively cancel the already-committed bullet). Killing the enemy (hp→0) strictly before that final tick sends it to `HIT`/`DEAD`; it never enters `SHOOTING` and spawns no bullet — the reward for winning the duel. Because `SHOOTING` is always preceded by ≥1 `AIMING` tick, the existing "pre-hit snapshot must not suppress a committed telegraphed shot" invariant holds, now honestly: the commit point is entry to `SHOOTING`, after the telegraph. During `AIMING` the enemy shows a crisis-flash (~7Hz pulse on weapon/silhouette, categorically distinct from the steady exposure rim) — read spec only, visual is lead-art's. This pulse is exempt from AC16's ≤3 full-opacity-flashes/sec global cap because it is a small-area cue strictly bounded to the enemy's own screen-space silhouette (not a full-screen or dominant-area flash), consistent with WCAG 2.3.1's small-area exception; lead-art must keep the pulse's flashing area within that per-enemy bound. Under `Prefs.reducedMotion === true`, the crisis-flash pulse is replaced by a steady non-flashing bright highlight held for the `AIMING` duration (no flashing) — see AC17 — the telegraph's timing is unaffected, only its flashing quality is tempered. During `AIMING`, the exposure rim (`neonHeatColor.ts`, ADR-0025) holds frozen at its value from the moment `VISIBLE` ends — it does not keep climbing and does not reset to green — so the two cues stay categorically distinct (rim = frozen exposure-at-telegraph-onset marker, crisis-flash = the new imminent-fire read); `neonHeatColor.ts` needs a passthrough/frozen case for `AIMING`.

**Multi-magnitude shake + hitstop.** No shake/hitstop exists anywhere in gameplay today. Add one pure per-tick field `GameState.shake: { magnitude: 'small' | 'medium' | 'large'; hitstopMs: number } | null` — the strongest impulse resolved this tick, computed pure. Tier map:
- Miss, or a hit on a still-alive enemy (hp>1) → `small`, no hitstop.
- Kill (→`DEAD`) → `medium` + hitstop (`hitstopKill`).
- A shot resolves as an "innocent" penalty outcome (`shot.livesDelta < 0`; currently only the street Courier — the window-enemy `civilian` type is art-retired/weight-0 per ADR-0029 and out of scope here, but keying the tier on the penalty signal rather than literally on "courier" means it correctly covers civilian too if that type is ever re-enabled) → `large` + hitstop (`hitstopError`, longest).
- Player hit by an enemy bullet → `medium`, **no hitstop** (never freeze the frame the player is punished on — keep recovery snappy).

**Same-tier tie-break.** When two impulses tie at the same magnitude tier with different `hitstopMs` in the same tick (the concrete case: a kill resolves `medium+hitstopKill` while the player is also hit `medium+0` in that same tick), `hitstopMs` resolves to `0` — the no-hitstop value wins the tie. A player-punishment tick must never freeze, even when a kill also resolved that tick; magnitude for that tick stays `medium`. This is the one deliberate exception to "strongest wins": hitstop tie-breaks toward the player-hit's zero-freeze rule, never toward the kill's freeze.

**Cross-tick overlap.** If a new shake impulse arrives while the previous tick's decaying camera offset/hitstop hasn't finished, the new impulse's offset **replaces** the in-flight decay (reset-and-decay-from-the-new-impulse), never sums/stacks across ticks — a bridge-side (`useGameLoop.ts`) rule, not a pure-layer one.

`ImpactEvent` (`feedback.ts`) stays cosmetic-only and untouched; shake is its own signal. The bridge (`useGameLoop.ts`) reads `next.shake`: (a) applies a decaying random world-space offset to `camera.position` **after** `crosshairToWorld` has already resolved from the base pan/QTE position, so aim and the crosshair stay 1:1 with the pointer — the scene moves under a still crosshair (veille §2.3 hard rule); (b) runs a hitstop countdown that holds the sim (freezes enemy timers + bullets, skips advancing `tickGameState`) for `hitstopMs` while rendering + shake decay continue — the hold is **tick-based, not delta-based**: the bridge withholds `tickGameState` for at least one full skipped frame regardless of that frame's `safeDelta` (capped at `MAX_DELTA=0.1s`), so a 60-80ms (or 20ms reduced-motion) hitstop is never swallowed or collapsed by a single slow/low-fps frame. Input received during an active hitstop is buffered and applied on the tick hitstop resumes — it must not be silently discarded. Boundary law: magnitude + hitstop duration are pure game values; camera displacement + tick-hold live only in the hooks/render bridge. Camera-write ownership: the bridge (`useGameLoop.ts`) is the **single** writer of `camera.position` (base pan/QTE pose + additive shake); `GameScene.tsx` must not independently write `camera.position` for shake — one owner avoids a two-writer frame-ordering race and guarantees `crosshairToWorld` reads the un-shaken base (AC8). The device fork for `touchTelegraphMs` (AC18) must never be read inside `src/game` — `enemySystem.ts`/`enemyTypes.ts` must not read `IS_MOBILE` or `window.matchMedia` directly; the bridge resolves ADR-0003's `IS_MOBILE` and **injects** the resolved telegraph duration as a parameter into the pure tick function, the same way `levelParams`/roster are already injected (boundary law, see `src/game/systems/narrativeSystem.ts:73`).

**Impact + death grammar (partly done — refine).** Impact white flash exists twice today: `EnemySprite` HIT-state white burst (scale 1.6) held the full `HIT_DURATION` = 200ms, plus `ImpactEffects` categorical white flash at 33ms (~2 frames). The 200ms solid white reads as a sustained tint, not a flash. Refine: trim the struck-silhouette flash to ≤2 frames (≤~50ms) before it reverts to pose/death-break; keep the 33ms categorical flash as-is. Death = on hp→0 (`HIT`→`DEAD`) the killed silhouette must visibly topple/drop (render-side plane rotate ~75° + drop + fade over `deathBreakMs`) before clearing — never an instant blink-out. Failure feedback on an innocent hit must be ultra-legible and categorically distinct from a normal miss: `large` shake + 80ms hitstop + a dedicated failure cue (period-faithful B&W print stamp, e.g. "BAVURE"/"RATÉ", + distinct SFX) — not merely the existing `-1♥` floater. The stamp is a print/HUD surface (lead-art/ux jurisdiction); this story owns only the read contract: an innocent hit must be identifiable as categorically different within one glance, driven by the `large` tier + failure cue, no second colour added (art-direction §2/§8.1).

## Tuning (à playtester, non gaté)

| Parameter | Start value | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `telegraphMs` (AIMING duration = riposte window) | 400 ms, shared across shooters | Human simple reaction ~250ms + aim travel. Shared/one-value first (YAGNI); per-archetype override is a documented follow-up, not shipped here. One-variable rule: land this, re-measure pacing, then rebalance exposure durations. |
| `touchTelegraphMs` (mobile override) | 700 ms | Veille §2.4 / ADR-0015: touch needs a longer telegraph (bigger hitbox, slower aim). Floor: ≥1.3× the desktop value for the same archetype/difficulty. |
| `shakeSmall` | amplitude 0.04 world, 90 ms | Per-shot "kick" (miss / non-lethal hit). Barely-there so rapid fire doesn't nauseate. |
| `shakeMedium` (kill / player-hit) | amplitude 0.12 world, 160 ms | The satisfying "confirm" on a takedown; also the damage-taken jolt. |
| `shakeLarge` (innocent / error) | amplitude 0.22 world, 240 ms | Biggest — the mistake must feel bad. Paired with the failure stamp. |
| `hitstopKill` | 60 ms | Low end of the veille's 60-80ms band — kills are frequent, keep the freeze crisp. |
| `hitstopError` | 80 ms | Top of the band — the innocent-hit mistake earns the longest pause. Player-hit gets no hitstop by design. |
| `shakeFrequency` | 40 Hz | Rapid random offset both axes; reads as a jolt, not a wobble. |
| `shakeDecayCurve` | linear-to-zero | Simplest first; trauma² (Eiserloh) is an optional follow-up if the falloff reads too mechanical. |
| `whiteFlashMs` (struck silhouette) | ≤50 ms (~2 frames) | Trim `EnemySprite` HIT white from 200ms; keep `ImpactEffects` 33ms categorical flash as-is. |
| `deathBreakMs` (topple) | 180 ms | Render-side topple+drop+fade on `DEAD` before clearing. |

## Out of scope (V1)
